## The problem

Cyber insurance applications ask detailed questions about security controls: multi-factor authentication, backups, endpoint protection, patching, staff training and incident response. Answers are often rushed at renewal time, and an answer that doesn't match reality can cause trouble later if a claim is made.

## What's in the pack

- **Application draft**: the renewal questions answered from the facts you or your MSP provide, with every answer I can't confirm flagged for review
- **Evidence summary**: for each control question, what proves it (for example, a screenshot of MFA settings or a backup report), so answers can be backed up if asked
- **Gap list**: controls the application asks about that aren't in place yet, with what to fix before renewal
- **One-page incident response plan**, if the business doesn't have one
