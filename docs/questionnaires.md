## The problem

Enterprise and mid-market buyers send a security questionnaire before they sign: a spreadsheet, a SIG Lite or CAIQ-style form, or a vendor portal with 50 to 300 questions. Founders end up answering it at midnight, guessing at wording, and promising controls that don't exist yet. Wrong answers can lose the deal or come back later as a contract problem.

## What I do

- Answer every question from facts you give me: a short intake form or call, your website, your cloud setup and any existing documents
- Write answers that are accurate today and say plainly what is planned, rather than overstating
- Flag every question I can't answer from your facts, so nothing is invented
- Point to the policy or practice that backs each answer
- Give you the answers as a reusable library, so the next questionnaire takes hours, not days
