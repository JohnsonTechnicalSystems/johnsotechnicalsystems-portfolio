For AI companies and software companies that use AI, whose customers now ask how their data is used with models, and whether you have an AI policy.

## The problem

Security questionnaires now include an AI section. It asks whether customer data is used to train models, which third-party model providers you use, who can see prompts and outputs, and how you review AI output. Without a written policy, every answer is improvised, and answers drift from one customer to the next.

## What I do

<div class="jts-ledger" markdown>

- **AI use policy** How your team and your product use AI, which providers are approved, what data may go to them, and how output is reviewed
- **AI tool inventory** A simple list of the AI tools and model providers your team and product use, what data each one touches, and who approved it
- **Ready answers** For the AI section of customer questionnaires, consistent with the policy
- **Light ISO/IEC 42001 gap check (optional)** A short list, in plain English, of where your current practices stand against the main themes of ISO/IEC 42001, the international standard for AI management systems. This is a readiness view, not a certification audit.
