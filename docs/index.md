## How it works

1. **Send what you have.** The questionnaire, plus anything already written: your website, existing policies, a short call or a few emailed answers about how you run things.
2. **I draft and verify.** Every answer is written from your facts. Anything I can't confirm is flagged for you, never invented. Framework references are checked against the source text.
3. **You review and send.** You get the completed file, a short list of open questions, and reusable answers for the next review.
