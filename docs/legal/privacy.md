# Privacy

Last updated: November 2026. This site is run by johnsontechnicalsystems LLC.

## This website

- The site has **no analytics, no tracking cookies and no contact forms.**
- It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security and operations; see the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- The site remembers your light or dark mode choice in your browser's local storage. That stays on your device.

## When you email me

I use your name, email address and what you send only to reply and to do the work you ask for. I don't sell or share it, and I don't add you to a mailing list.

## Client documents

Documents shared for a job are handled as described on the [Security page](../security.md) and deleted at the end of the job unless our agreement says otherwise.
