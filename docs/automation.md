## The problem

Small clinics run on a practice management system plus email, phone, forms and spreadsheets. The gaps between them are filled by hand: confirming appointments, chasing missing records, sending aftercare instructions, answering the same questions again and again.

## What I build

Small, specific automations around the tools you already use, for example:

- Drafting reminder, follow-up and aftercare emails for staff to review and send
- Turning intake forms into a clean summary before the appointment
- A daily brief of what needs attention: unanswered messages, missing records, tomorrow's schedule gaps

Every build keeps a person in the loop for anything sent to a client, and starts with a short review of what data the automation may touch.
