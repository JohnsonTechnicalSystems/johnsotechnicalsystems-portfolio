# Security: how I handle your documents

You will be sharing security details about your company. This page says what happens to them.

## Before any work

- **NDA by default.** I sign your NDA, or offer a mutual NDA, before you share anything confidential.
- **Partners** also get a subcontractor agreement that covers confidentiality and non-solicitation of their clients.
- **Ask only for what's needed.** I work from descriptions, policies and screenshots. I don't need, and won't accept, passwords, keys or access to your production systems.

## During the work

- **One folder per client or partner,** in encrypted cloud storage, shared only with the people you name.
- **Accounts protected with multi-factor authentication,** on a device with full-disk encryption and automatic screen lock.
- **Email** through Proton Mail. For sensitive files, I'll send a shared-folder link rather than an attachment.
- **AI tools** help with first drafts. I don't paste confidential client details into AI tools that use inputs for training, and I'll tell you which tools I use if you ask.

## At the end of the job

- **Deletion.** Your source documents are deleted at the end of the job, normally within 30 days, unless our agreement says otherwise. Delivered files are yours.
- **Confirmation on request.** I'll confirm in writing when deletion is done.

## If something goes wrong

If I learn that your documents may have been exposed, I'll tell you within 72 hours of finding out, with what I know.
